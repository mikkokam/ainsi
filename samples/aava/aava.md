##### Harbour, Kotka

# Eleven days on the quay, one usable frame a day

The brief asked for machinery. What the client bought was six frames of people waiting, because the machinery does nothing anyone can photograph and the waiting is the whole job.

Shot on a 35mm at f/2 and available light throughout. No lighting on site, no releases beyond the crew, and the ship moved twice.

# How a shoot runs

Four things, every time

---

##### The short version

<!-- ainsi: columns -->
1. **A brief in one paragraph** if it takes a page, we have not agreed what the picture is
2. **A recce, or photographs of the room** light is the whole decision and it is made before the day
3. **One direction, shot properly** two half-covered directions is how a day is wasted
4. **The edit is mine** you pick from a selection; you do not sit in the edit

<!-- ainsi: end -->

> [!NOTE]
> A day is nine hours on site including setup. Beyond that is a second half day, agreed on the day, never invoiced as a surprise.
